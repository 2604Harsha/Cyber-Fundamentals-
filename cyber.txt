Cyber Security Fundamentals & Basic Security Answers

1.	Explain What Cyber Security and Why it is Important 
Ans) Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, applications, devices, 
and data from unauthorized access, attacks, theft, and damage. It is important because it protects sensitive
 information, prevents cyberattacks and financial losses, maintains privacy, ensures system availability, and 
 builds trust between organizations and their customers 

2.	Explain at least 5 common cyber threats such as phishing, Malware, Ransomware, Social Engineering, and Password Attacks.
 Ans) Phishing: 
            Phishing is a Cyberattack where attackers send fake emails, messages, or 
            website that appear to be legitimate to steal sensitive information such as
             passwords, bank details or OTPs. 
    Malware: 
            Malware (Malicious Software) is software Designed to damage system, steal 
            information or gain authorized access 

    Common Types Include:
	Virus
	Worm 
	Trojan 
	Spyware 
	Adware 

    Ransomware:
         Ransomware is malware that encrypts or block access to files or systems and demands money from the victim to restore access. 
         Example: A company’s files become encrypted and a message demands payment in cryptocurrency to decrypt them. 

    Social Engineering:
        Social Engineering involves manipulating or deceiving people into revealing confidential information  or performing an unsafe action . 
        Example: An attacker pretends to be an IT Employee and asks a staff member for their password 

     Password Attacks: 
        Password attacks are attempts to obtain or guess a user’s password to gain unauthorized access. 
    Common methods include:
	   Brute – force attacks 
	   Dictionary Attacks 
	   Credential Stuffing 
	   Password Spraying 

3.	Explain the Difference Between a Strong Password and Weak Password 
Ans) Strong Password:
	Long and Complex 
	Uses Upper case, lowercase, numbers and special characters 
	Difficult to guess 
	Unique for each Account 
	Does not contain personal Information 
              	Weak Password:
	Short and Simple 
	Uses only letters or simple numbers 
	Easy to guess 
	Reused  across Multiple Accounts 
	Contains names, Birthdays, Phone numbers 

4.	List at least 7 basic Cyber Security Practices that every user should follow 
       Ans)  Every User Should Follow these basics
	Use strong and Unique Passwords
	Enable Multi Factor Authentication 
	Keep Software Updated 
	Be careful with Emails and Links 
	Use Antivirus and  Firewall Protection 
	Avoid Unsecure Public Wi Fi 
	Back Up important Data 

5.	Explain Why Multi Factor Authentication(MFA) is Important 
Ans)  Multi – Factor Authentication(MFA) is Important because  It provides  an additional
 layer of security beyond a password. Even if a password is stolen or compromised , the 
 attacker still needs another verification Factor, such as an OTP, authenticator app, or biometric Verification
